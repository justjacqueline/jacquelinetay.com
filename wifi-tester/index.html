Run a test to score this spot.

Latency -- Waiting How long each response takes. Lower feels more instant.
Jitter -- Waiting How jumpy the connection is. High jitter causes frozen or robotic calls.
Download -- Waiting How fast you receive video, shared screens, and files.
Upload -- Waiting How well your camera, voice, and screen share send out.

How to read this

Latency under 100 ms is usually fine for Zoom.

Jitter over 50 ms is the big warning sign for choppy calls.

Upload under 4 Mbps can make your video or screen share struggle.
