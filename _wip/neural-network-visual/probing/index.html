Two probes, two directions

Each dot below is a pretend model activation. Sliders change the data; the visualization shows two ways of reading a "truth" direction off it — and how they can disagree.

The y-axis lines up with whether something is true; the x-axis is a nuisance feature like topic, template, wording, or number size. Blue dots are true examples, orange dots are false. Two probe directions are drawn as arrows, plus the logistic classifier's decision boundary.

Tip: hover the small i next to any slider for an explanation. The axes are fixed, so the points really do move as you change the sliders.
