The dot product: a probe meets an activation

Once you have a probe (a direction in activation space), how do you actually use it on a model activation? You compute the dot product. Geometrically, that's how much of the activation lines up with the probe — the length of its shadow on the probe direction.

The probe is constrained to unit length (it's a direction, not a magnitude). The gold segment is the activation's shadow on the probe — and its signed length is the dot product. Inspired by Falstad's classic dot product applet.
